01 / Customer journey

From redirection friction to an in-app partner experience.

The earlier partner flow depended on a redirect / WebView experience. We changed the model: expose the right backend APIs, let partner applications host the journey natively, and remove a layer of friction the customer never asked for.

41% → 75%

Journey completion after moving toward a native API-led partner experience.

Less redirecting. More completing.

~4K → 12K

Monthly transactions scaled during the period I worked on the product.

02 / Scale

Growth was not one feature. It was a lot of small removals of friction.

Journey improvements. Product fixes. Operational bottlenecks. Platform capabilities. Post-launch iteration. The product scaled because the system around the customer kept getting better.

03 / Systems

I think about how the full system behaves: what the customer sees, what the platform decides, and what operations inherits when the two do not agree.

How I think

A working set of product instincts.

Not a framework. More like the questions that keep returning.

  1. I usually start by asking what problem we are actually solving. The ticket is not always the problem.

    Start one layer below the request.

  2. I care more about the objective than defending the first solution. Attachment is expensive.

    Keep the goal. Re-negotiate the route.

  3. Customer experience is shaped by systems, operations, compliance and architecture. The interface only tells part of the story.

    A clean screen can still hide a broken product.

  4. I look for the smallest change that can unlock the largest improvement. Small and easy are not the same thing.

    Leverage beats theatre.

  5. Shipping ends the debate and begins the evidence. Behaviour after launch is when the product starts teaching you things.

    The roadmap does not get the final word.
