Selected work

Three product problems. Three very different shapes.

Public-safe portfolio version. Real work, real metrics, and enough context to show how I think — without dumping confidential internals onto the page.

01 / Customer journey

From redirect friction to a native partner experience.

The earlier partner flow depended on a redirect / WebView experience. We changed the model: expose the right backend APIs, let partner applications host the journey natively, and remove a layer of friction the customer never asked for.

51% → 75% Journey completion after moving toward a native API-led partner experience.
~4K→12K
Monthly transactions scaled during the period I worked on the product.
02 / Scale

Growth was not one feature. It was a lot of small removals of friction.

Journey improvements. Product fixes. Operational bottlenecks. Platform capabilities. Post-launch iteration. The product scaled because the system around the customer kept getting better.

03 / Systems

The customer should not have to understand the backend.

Financial products sit across authentication, pricing, core banking, partner platforms, transaction systems, compliance checks and operations. My job is to understand that complexity without exporting it to the customer.

How I think

The door is not the problem.

If the goal is to get to the other side, breaking the door is just one option. Sometimes it is right. Sometimes it is lazy. I like looking at the whole system before choosing the route.

Objective: get to the other side
A
Break the doorThe obvious answer. Sometimes correct. Sometimes just familiar.
B
Question the constraintDo we need the door? Is the wall easier? Can the journey be redesigned?
C
Find the lower-resistance routeJump. Go around. Dig. Remove the wall. Use what actually works.
D
Then measure itA clever answer that does not improve the outcome is still the wrong answer.
